MODE: REPURPOSE an existing session.

The CURRENT DOC TO REVISE section below holds the source doc. The AUTHOR'S CHANGE
BRIEF states what to REMOVE and what to ADD.

Rules for this mode, in priority order:

1. Remove exactly what the brief says to remove. Do not keep a trimmed trace of
   it "for context" -- removed means gone, including from the Recap, the flow
   summary and What's Next.
2. Add exactly what the brief says to add, built to the seven rules like any new
   material: explained before shown, real outputs only, cited.
3. Keep the rest intact, including its real outputs.
4. After removals and additions, re-check the through-line. The hook must still
   lead to what the session now teaches, and the flow summary must match the
   steps that remain. Repair breaks caused by the edit and say so.
5. At the end, under a `CHANGES MADE` heading, list removals and additions
   separately, one line each.
